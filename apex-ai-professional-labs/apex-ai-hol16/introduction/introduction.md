# Extending Application Capabilities

## Introduction

ESS and TAP are fully styled and ready to use. This module extends both applications with native APEX components, the approved Signature Capture plug-in, scheduled automations, and reusable email templates.

ESS is the primary application. You create a leave calendar, a 30-Day Check-In survey, email templates, scheduled automations, and an administrator System Logs page. In TAP, you create an interview calendar, retain offer-signature capture, create email templates, and automate interview and offer notifications.

### Objectives

- Create native Calendar pages in ESS and TAP.
- Build the ESS 30-Day Check-In survey with native Star Rating items.
- Configure Signature Capture for pending TAP offers.
- Create scheduled automations, email templates, and an ESS automation log report.
- Review the APEX translation workflow.

Estimated Workshop Time: 60 minutes
