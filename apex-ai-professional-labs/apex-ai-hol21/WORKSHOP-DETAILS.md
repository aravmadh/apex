# Workshop Details

Estimated Time: 5 minutes

## Short Description

Build routed and parallel Oracle APEX workflows that automate requisition approval and employee onboarding across TAP and ESS.

## Long Description

Business processes often require people to review, approve, and complete work before the next step can begin. In this workshop, you use Oracle APEX Workflow and Human Tasks to make those decisions visible, actionable, and auditable.

In TAP, you route submitted job requisitions by headcount and update their status after approval or rejection. In ESS, you create parallel onboarding tasks for HR and the employee's department manager, then send a welcome email only after both tasks are complete.

## Workshop Outline

1. Introduction
2. Lab 1 - TAP Requisition Approval Workflow
3. Lab 2 - ESS Employee Onboarding Workflow

## Workshop Prerequisites

- Existing TAP and ESS applications with the TMS tables and authorization schemes referenced in the labs.
- Access to APEX Shared Components and the required application roles.
- A configured outbound mail environment and the existing ESS welcome email template.

- The supplied source allocates 3 hours 45 minutes to Module 21. It provides full procedures only for the two labs in this workshop; the offer approval and leave approval flows named in the source need SME-authored build steps before publication.
